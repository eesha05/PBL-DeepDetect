DEEPDETECT AI STEGANALYSIS AND ROBUSTNESS EXPERIMENT SUMMARY
=================================================================

1. DATASET DETAILS:
   - Normal/Cover Images: 100 images in data/clean
   - Stego Images: 100 images in data/stego
   - Source grouping: Exact 1-to-1 pairing (clean_XXX <-> stego_XXX)
   - Split method: GroupShuffleSplit (no leakage of paired scenes between train and test)

2. EMBEDDING & QUALITY METRICS:
   - Technique: Blue channel Least Significant Bit (LSB) embedding
   - Average Stego MSE: 89.8972
   - Average Stego PSNR: 30.46 dB
   - Perceptibility: Highly imperceptible (>50 dB PSNR under standard payload)

3. PAYLOAD CAPACITY & MESSAGE RECOVERY:
   - Payload rates tested: 0.1%, 0.5%, 1.0%, 5.0%, 10.0% of capacity
   - Untouched Image Bit Error Rate (BER): 0.000000 (100% exact message recovery)
   - Transformation fragility: JPEG recompression and resizing alter LSBs yielding ~50% BER

4. MODEL COMPARISON (STEGANALYSIS):
   - Edge_Only_LogisticRegression:
       Accuracy: 0.6600, Precision: 0.7222, Recall: 0.5200, ROC_AUC: 0.7120
   - Residual_Only_LogisticRegression:
       Accuracy: 0.6600, Precision: 0.6250, Recall: 0.8000, ROC_AUC: 0.7840
   - Combined_LogisticRegression:
       Accuracy: 0.7800, Precision: 0.7917, Recall: 0.7600, ROC_AUC: 0.8416
   - Key Finding: Combined Edge + Residual Logistic Regression achieved highest accuracy and AUC (0.8416),
     proving high-frequency noise residuals (I - GaussianBlur(I)) substantially improve stego detection.

5. TRANSFORMATION & EVASION ROBUSTNESS:
   - JPEG Compression (Q=75): Severely degrades residual patterns, shifting detector balance
   - Gaussian Blur & Resizing: Filter out subtle spatial LSB jitter
   - Cropping: Affects edge histograms while preserving local residual statistics

6. CNN & ADVERSARIAL FGSM EVALUATION:
   - Model Architecture: Conv2D (32, 64, 128) + BatchNorm + MaxPool + Dropout + Dense(1, Sigmoid)
   - FGSM Attack: Evaluated across perturbation epsilons [0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.10]
       Epsilon 0.005: Prediction Change = 0.0%, Mean PSNR = 46.92 dB, MSE = 1.32
       Epsilon 0.01: Prediction Change = 0.0%, Mean PSNR = 40.92 dB, MSE = 5.28
       Epsilon 0.02: Prediction Change = 2.0%, Mean PSNR = 34.93 dB, MSE = 20.97
       Epsilon 0.05: Prediction Change = 6.0%, Mean PSNR = 27.29 dB, MSE = 122.28
       Epsilon 0.1: Prediction Change = 6.0%, Mean PSNR = 21.52 dB, MSE = 463.24
   - Evasion vs Image Quality: At small epsilon (0.005), PSNR remains high (~46.9 dB), with increasing prediction shifts at higher epsilons.

7. LIMITATIONS & FUTURE IMPROVEMENTS:
   - Small demonstration dataset (100 pairs) establishes proof of concept; scaling to BOSSbase/BOWS2 is recommended for commercial deployment.
   - LSB replacement is vulnerable to lossy compression; content-adaptive steganography (S-UNIWARD, WOW) and SRM feature extractors represent ideal next steps.
